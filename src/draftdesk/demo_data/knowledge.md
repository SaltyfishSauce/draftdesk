# Maple Lane Goods: support policies

## Shipping
- Orders are packed within 2 business days of purchase.
- Standard delivery in the US takes 3 to 6 business days after dispatch. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days.
- A tracking link is emailed when the order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show movement.
- If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, we open a trace with the carrier.

## Returns and refunds
- Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund to the original payment method.
- Return shipping is free for US orders. Start a return at https://maplelane.example/returns
- Refunds are issued within 5 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse.

## Damaged or faulty items
- Ask the customer for a photo of the damage and the order number.
- We offer a replacement or a full refund, whichever the customer prefers. A support agent must approve it.

## Sizing
- Jackets run true to size. Customers between sizes are advised to size up for layering.
- Full size charts: https://maplelane.example/size-guide

## Contact
- Support hours: Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Eastern Time.
- Email: support@maplelane.example
